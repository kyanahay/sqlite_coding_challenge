# SQL Sales Analysis Insights

## Customer Spending
Jacob Foster is the top-spending customer, with a total lifetime spend of $8,722.67. Ethan Gomez follows closely with $8,206.19. This suggests that a small number of high-value customers contribute heavily to total sales.

There is a noticeable drop after the top two customers, with the remaining top five customers ranging from $4,409.83 to $5,471.57. This shows a partially top-heavy revenue distribution, where retaining top customers should be a priority.

## Product Category Revenue
Electronics is the highest-grossing product category, generating $25,364.23 in revenue. This makes it the primary revenue driver in the dataset.

Furniture generated $12,712.00, which is roughly half of Electronics revenue. This makes Furniture a strong secondary contributor.

Lower-performing categories such as Grocery ($405.72) and Stationery ($319.24) contribute very little to overall revenue. These categories may need stronger marketing, bundling, or pricing adjustments if the business wants to increase their impact.

## Employee Salary Analysis
Each department has at least one employee earning above the department average. This confirms that salary distribution varies within every department.

The Sales department has the largest gap between an individual salary and the department average, at approximately $11,000. This may suggest performance-based compensation, seniority differences, or uneven pay distribution.

The IT department also shows a notable gap of approximately $6,600, while other departments have smaller differences between individual salaries and department averages.

## Customer Loyalty by City
Tampa has the highest number of Gold-tier customers, with 4 Gold customers. This makes Tampa the strongest city for high-value or loyal customers.

Gold customers are not evenly distributed across all cities. This indicates that loyalty is geographically concentrated, which may help guide future marketing or customer retention efforts.

## Business Takeaway
The business should prioritize retaining top-spending customers, continue investing in Electronics as the strongest revenue category, and investigate opportunities to grow weaker categories. Tampa may also be a strong market for loyalty campaigns, upselling, and targeted promotions.
